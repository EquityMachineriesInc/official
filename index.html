Our Story

Equity Machineries, Inc. (EMI) traces its roots back to 1949 under the leadership of Isabelo and Illuminada Tactay, who operated the Cauayan Electric Corporation in Cauayan, Isabela.

1949
Roots in Energy & Farming: Known as "Kumpanyang Mapagkakatiwalaan," the Tactay family expanded into large-scale farming using Caterpillar and Fordson tractors alongside mechanized threshers.
1972
A Shift in Direction: During Martial Law, when private power plants were placed under government control, the family focused on agriculture and established Isabela Machineries.
1975
Birth of EMI: On March 12, 1975, Isabela Machineries was renamed Equity Machineries, Incorporated under the leadership of Atty. Bagnos B. Tactay and Ma. Carayo Tactay-Perez.
